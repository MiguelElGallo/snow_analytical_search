[`data/` folder at the pinned revision](https://github.com/Azure-Samples/azure-search-openai-demo/tree/558ffd397eddcfe4da5c85ebc285796b367eb9b2/data)

[Azure-Samples/azure-search-openai-demo](https://github.com/Azure-Samples/azure-search-openai-demo)

The downloader preserves the original file bytes and saves the upstream
`LICENSE` as `UPSTREAM_LICENSE` alongside the local corpus. Ingestion uploads
those originals to the configured Snowflake stage. Parsed pages and chunks
are stored separately. Documentation contains small source-derived examples
and attribution links.
